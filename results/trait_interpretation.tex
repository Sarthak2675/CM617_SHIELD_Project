The independent $2\times2$ trait ablation prevents the original correlated archetypes from being interpreted as an isolated test of drought tolerance. At fixed $K_p=0.0055$, changing $\psi_{50}$ from $-1.4$ to $-3.0$ MPa changes $C$ from 163.35 to 168.36 K h and final-day cooling from 1.98 to 1.84 K. At fixed $K_p=0.008$, changing $\psi_{50}$ from $-1.4$ to $-3.0$ MPa changes $C$ from 165.67 to 166.50 K h and final-day cooling from 1.91 to 1.63 K. The response is therefore an interaction between water transport and the evolving soil store, rather than an effect that can be assigned solely to the archetype label. Full values are in \texttt{independent\_traits.csv}.
