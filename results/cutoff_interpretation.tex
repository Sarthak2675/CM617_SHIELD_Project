With the SC bookkeeping floor moved from $-6$ to $-4$ MPa, final-day cooling changes from 1.84 to 1.63 K; moving it to $-8$ MPa gives 1.84 K. The default SC run ends at -5.85 MPa. Thus the LAI 3 daily-irrigation comparison is insensitive to extending the floor to $-8$ MPa, but a shallower allowable store changes the answer. This sensitivity is reported rather than treating the chosen floor as a biological fact. See \texttt{cutoff\_audit.csv}.
